Climate
Mild oceanic climate — 8–17°C through the year, rarely freezing, with the warming pull of the Gulf Stream.
Sunshine & Daylight
Around 1,640 hours of sun a year — among the sunniest spots in Britain — and 16+ hour midsummer days.
Air Quality
Clean Atlantic air — low PM2.5 and nitrogen dioxide, well inside WHO guideline limits year-round.
Coastal & Marine
Three Blue Flag beaches — Porthmeor, Porthminster and Carbis Bay — with ~6.5m tidal range and summer seas near 16°C.
Wind
Breezy and exposed — prevailing south-westerlies off the Atlantic average 12–15 mph, easing in sheltered harbour streets.
Local Context
A historic artists' town — Tate St Ives, the Barbara Hepworth garden, narrow granite lanes and a working fishing harbour.
