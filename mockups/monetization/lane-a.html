What this means for you

This is a climate built for living outdoors. Summers stay comfortable rather than hot — peaking around 20 °C — while winters rarely turn truly cold, with means holding near 8–9 °C and frost an occasional guest rather than a season.

The narrow annual swing keeps gardens green and growing for much of the year, and the long Atlantic light gives St Ives some of the brightest skies in Britain. Expect wetter, breezier spells from late autumn through winter — and unusually gentle, golden conditions the rest of the year.
