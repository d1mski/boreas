Climate

Mild and oceanic: comfortable nearly all year, with some of Britain's best sunshine. Winters stay gentle, summers stay fresh, and frost is a rare visitor on this stretch of the Atlantic coast.

What this means for day-to-day life

You can expect comfortable layers rather than extremes: heating bills stay modest through gentle winters, and summers stay pleasant without the heat that tires inland towns. Wetter, breezier spells arrive in late autumn (good waterproofs earn their keep from October to December), but long bright evenings and a frost-free garden are the trade you get for living beside the Atlantic. For most families, it simply means more time spent outdoors, comfortably, across more of the year.
