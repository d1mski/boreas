Assessment

A 9°C annual swing and near-absent frost make St Ives one of the most temperate locations in Britain: comfortable year-round, with mild winters that protect tender planting and extend the gardening season. Summers stay warm rather than hot (means peaking at 17°C), favouring outdoor living without heat stress. Rainfall concentrates in late autumn, while spring and early summer stay notably dry, supporting consistent outdoor and amenity use.
